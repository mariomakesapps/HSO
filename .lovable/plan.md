## The Plan — 4 Phases

### Phase 1: Fix indexing & on-page basics (Week 1)

1. **Investigate 231 not-indexed pages**
   - Go to GSC > Pages > Not indexed
   - Identify the 4 reasons
   - Fix the biggest bucket first (likely soft 404s, duplicate content, or crawl budget issues)

2. **Retitle opportunity pages for search intent**
   - Current: "JHU Pre-College | HSO"
   - Target: "Johns Hopkins Pre-College Summer Program for High School Students | HSO"
   - Include the category keyword naturally in every `<title>` and `<h1>`
   - Same for meta descriptions — mention eligibility, grades, cost, deadline

3. **Retitle category pages**
   - Current: generic category names
   - Target: "High School Internships 2026 | Engineering, Research & STEM | HSO"
   - These are your highest-potential pages because they aggregate many opportunities

### Phase 2: Add real content to category & collection pages (Week 2–3)

The SERP shows guides and listicles rank. Your category pages are just filtered grids — Google sees them as thin.

For each major category page, add above-the-grid editorial content:

- What kinds of internships exist for high schoolers
- How to choose the right one (by grade, interest, location)
- When to apply (timeline advice)
- How to stand out (1–2 tips)
- FAQ section with 3–5 common questions (e.g., "Can freshmen get internships?", "Are high school internships paid?")

This turns thin filter pages into substantive guides that Google wants to rank.

### Phase 3: Build internal linking architecture (Week 3–4)

- Every opportunity page should link to its parent category with keyword-rich anchor text (not just "Back")
- Related opportunities should link across categories (e.g., a research program links to "More research programs for high school students")
- Collections should link to relevant categories and vice versa
- Add a "Popular searches" or "Students also look for" section on the homepage with text links to category pages

### Phase 4: Earn backlinks (Ongoing)

This is the #1 reason Standout Search and .edu sites outrank you. Backlinks = authority in Google's eyes.

Tactics:
- **Guest posts** on education blogs (TeenLife, CollegeVine, Niche)
- **Resource page link building** — find high school counseling websites with resource lists, email them to add HSO
- **Original data/research** — publish stats like "Average cost of high school summer programs by state" and pitch it to journalists
- **Program partnerships** — ask the organizations you list to link back to their HSO page as a "verified listing"
- **Local news** — pitch HSO as a local story to newspapers in major metro areas
